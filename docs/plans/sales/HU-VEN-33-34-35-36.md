## API (`apps/api/src/modules/sales`)

| Method | Route | Permission | Behaviour | Error codes |

| PUT | `sales/pos/checkout/items/order` | `sales.pos.use` | Body `{ itemIds: uuid[] }`. - With "Descuento (redondeado)", a rounding upwards makes the row smaller than the customer's nominal discount; the exact amounts stay stored separately (`discount_amount`, `rounding_amount`).
- The quote editor loses the `rs-doc-table`; its existing unit and E2E tests must be rewritten for the list.
- Figma's shortcut bar also shows "F8 Descuento" (retired) and "Esc Cancelar venta" (not built); only the reorder notice is added.

## As built (12 Nov 2026)

What changed against this plan while building:

- **Added:** the return of a credit sale pays out only the money collected, and returns and cancellations report `creditReduction` and `creditReversal` (a cash refund of money never received was possible); `GET /sales/customers/:id/credit-balance`, derived from the sales; an empty state and focus keeping on the quote editor list.
- **Different:** the grip is an inline SVG of six dots in `rs-drag-handle`, not a new icon glyph; the three `measuresText` helpers of the ERP were not merged into `formatMeasures` (their formats differ); `rs-receipt` items gained an optional `note` line; the credit panel's due date is written "21 nov 2026" as Figma draws it, while the sale detail and the ticket keep dd/mm/aaaa; backlog statuses are now updated (they were not by earlier stories).
- **Tests:** `pos-credit-payment.int.spec.ts` also covers returns, cancellation, a dropped QR, negative stock and the balance; `equivalence-link.page.spec.ts` (inventory) now waits for its request instead of sleeping, because it failed under load.
- Full reference of what exists now: "As built" in `docs/sales/design-notes.md`.
